## 📚 Peer-Reviewed Scientific Publications

### 🏆 01. Rank-Collapse and Detail Re-injection for Efficient Safety-Helmet Detection
* **Authors:** **Hoa Van Duong**, Thuan Gia Nguyen, Thinh Quang Nguyen, Long Thanh Nguyen, Hoang Tran Ngoc
* **Venue:** *Multi-disciplinary Trends in Artificial Intelligence (MIWAI)*, Springer Nature (LNCS/LNAI)
* **Publisher Link:** [Springer Nature Link (Chapter 29)](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-981-92-6510-7_29)
* **DOI:** [`10.1007/978-981-92-6510-7_29`](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-92-6510-7_29)
* **Repository Summary & Citation:** [01_safety_helmet_detection_rcdr](./01_safety_helmet_detection_rcdr)
* **Summary:** Proposes a stage-aware architectural modification (RCDR) for YOLO11n to optimize industrial labor safety monitoring on cost-constrained edge hardware (NVIDIA GTX 1650). Retaining stage P2 and replacing C3k2 mixers from P3 onwards with a reparameterizable low-rank spatial mixer and depthwise ECA rail reduces parameters by **13.14%**, FLOPs by **9.21%**, and accelerates throughput to **72.89 FPS** (+9.10%) while maintaining baseline accuracy on SHWD.
* **Access Notice:** *In compliance with academic publishing agreements with Springer Nature, only citations, abstracts, and official publisher links are distributed. Full-text PDFs are not hosted publicly in this repository.*
