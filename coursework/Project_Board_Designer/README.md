## 🛠️ Lab Exercises & Circuit Schematics

### 💡 Exercise 1: Bidirectional LED Running Light
* **Objective:** Design a digital circuit that controls 10 LEDs to light sequentially from left to right, then from right to left continuously. The running speed is adjustable by altering the Clock frequency.
* **Tinkercad Circuit Design:** [Tinkercad Project Link](https://www.tinkercad.com/things/grpJtOjH4ta-cs200558duongvanhoabt1)
* **Key Components:** Clock, 2× CD4017 Decade Counter, 7404 Hex Inverter, 7408 Quad AND Gate, 10 LEDs, 220Ω Resistors.

---

### 🔢 Exercise 2: Seven-Segment Display Counter (0–9)
* **Objective:** Design a digital circuit to display numbers from 0 to 9 sequentially on a seven-segment display. The count increments automatically with each clock pulse and resets back to 0 after reaching 9.
* **Tinkercad Circuit Design:** [Tinkercad Project Link](https://www.tinkercad.com/things/f4KdkjWIOwU-cs200558duongvanhoabt2)
* **Key Components:** Clock, 7490 Decade Counter, 7447 BCD-to-7-Segment Decoder, Seven-Segment Display, 220Ω Resistors.

---

### 🔣 Exercise 3: Even Number Counter (0, 2, 4, 6, 8)
* **Objective:** Design a digital logic circuit that filters inputs to display only the even numbers (0, 2, 4, 6, and 8) sequentially on a seven-segment display, repeating continuously.
* **Tinkercad Circuit Design:** [Tinkercad Project Link](https://www.tinkercad.com/things/1MwcbeFGMh3-cs200558duongvanhoabt3)
* **Key Components:** Clock, 7490 Decade Counter, 7447 BCD-to-7-Segment Decoder, 7404 Hex Inverter, 7408 Quad AND Gate, Seven-Segment Display, 220Ω Resistors.
